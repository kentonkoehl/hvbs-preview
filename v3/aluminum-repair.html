That F-150? Aluminum.

Ford moved the F-150 body to aluminum alloy for the 2015 model year. It’s a work truck. It gets hit. It shouldn’t get fixed like steel.

Not sure what yours is made of? Put the year, make and model on the estimate form and we’ll tell you.

Aluminum is not steel

Fix it like steel and you find out later. Each manufacturer writes its own aluminum repair procedure. This is what that work calls for.

  • Keep steel off itSteel particles on bare aluminum start corrosion. It shows up as bubbling paint months after the repair.
  • Its own spaceAluminum work belongs in an enclosed area, away from steel grinding.
  • Its own toolsSanders, hammers, welder, rivet gun. None of them touch steel.
  • The factory’s bookHow far it can be worked, when a part gets replaced, how it gets joined. The manufacturer decides, not the tech.

Done wrong

Steel contamination, and the corrosion that follows it. Structure that won’t handle the next crash the way it was engineered to.

Neither one shows up the day you pick the truck up.
