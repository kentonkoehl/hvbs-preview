# Proposed website redesign sample

An unsolicited design sample showing what a redesign could look like.

**Not affiliated with, authorized by, or operated by the business shown.
This is not their live website.**

Every page carries a visible notice saying so, plus `noindex, nofollow,
noarchive` and a robots.txt disallow, so it cannot appear in search results
or compete with the real business.

Photo and review slots are labeled placeholders, not real content.
