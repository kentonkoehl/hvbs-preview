What we repair

Everything below happens in this building. Family owned and operated, I-CAR Gold Class certified, BBB A+ rated.

Collision repair

From a bumper tap in a parking lot to a vehicle that came in on a truck.

A collision repair is a sequence, and skipping any part of it shows up later. We write the damage first, including the parts you cannot see from the outside, because what is behind a crumpled fender usually matters more than the fender. Then panels get repaired where repairing them is correct, and replaced where it is not.

Structural work is measured against the manufacturer's specification, not eyeballed. If a vehicle is out of spec it goes back on the equipment until it is in spec. Then the repaired area gets prepped, painted, blended and reassembled, and we check panel gaps and alignment before anyone calls you.

  • Panel repair and panel replacement
  • Structural and frame work, measured to specification
  • Hidden damage documented before the estimate is finalized
  • Reassembly with fit and alignment checked

Aluminum structural repair

The reason a lot of vehicles get sent here from somewhere else.

Aluminum is a separate discipline. It needs its own isolated clean room, its own dedicated tooling that never touches steel, and manufacturer-specific training, because a procedure that is correct on steel can destroy an aluminum panel. This shop holds I-CAR Gold Class recognition plus aluminum certification for NISSAN, INFINITI, GM, FORD, HYUNDAI, FIAT and JEEP.

  • NISSAN
  • INFINITI
  • GM
  • FORD
  • HYUNDAI
  • FIAT
  • JEEP

If you own a 2015 or newer Ford F-150, that truck has an aluminum alloy body. Ford made that change for the 2015 model year, and it means your truck needs a shop that is certified for aluminum.

Insurance claims

We deal with the carrier so you are not translating body shop language over the phone.

You pick the shop. That is your call, not the insurance company's, and it is worth knowing before someone tells you where to take your vehicle. Once it is here, we write the damage, photograph and document it, and send it to your carrier. If the carrier's number does not cover the repair the vehicle actually needs, we go back to them with the documentation instead of cutting corners to hit their figure.

Bring your claim number if you have one. If you have not filed yet, that is fine too, and we can look at the damage first so you know what you are dealing with before you decide.

  • Damage written, photographed and documented for the carrier
  • Supplements handled directly with the adjuster
  • You are told what is approved and what is not before work starts

Paint and refinishing

A repair that is obvious under sunlight is not a finished repair.

Most of paint work is the part nobody sees: cleaning, sanding, masking, priming. The color itself is matched to your vehicle, then blended into the adjacent panels so the eye does not catch a line where the new finish starts. After it cures we inspect the finish for dirt and texture before the vehicle goes back together.

  • Color matched to your vehicle, blended into surrounding panels
  • Full prep, prime and mask process
  • Spot and panel refinishing
  • Finish inspected after cure

Dent and bumper repair

Small jobs get the same process as big ones.

Door dings, creased fenders, hail dents, scuffed and cracked bumper covers. Plenty of this is repairable without replacing anything, which often costs less and takes less time. Bumper covers in particular are often repairable even when they look finished. Bring it by and we will tell you honestly whether it is a repair or a replacement.

  • Dents and creases in panels
  • Bumper cover scuffs, cracks and tears
  • Scrapes and scratches
  • Repair over replacement where repair is the right answer

Free written estimates

No charge, in writing, and no obligation.

Bring the vehicle in during business hours and we will look at it and write it up. You leave with the damage in writing, whether or not you decide to have the work done here. Nothing gets repaired until you authorize it in writing.

If it is raining, we do not do estimates. Water sitting on the panels hides dents and hides paint damage, and we would rather have you come back than write something we cannot stand behind.

What to expect

Your repair, step by step

So there are no surprises about where your vehicle is in the process.

  1. Drop off

    Bring the vehicle to 1831 Marietta Church Rd during business hours. We take your information, your claim number if you have one, and the story of what happened.

  2. Estimate

    We go over the vehicle and write up the damage, including what is behind the panels. You get it in writing, free, before anything is decided.

  3. Insurance approval

    If a claim is involved, the estimate and the documentation go to your carrier. If they come back short of what the repair needs, we go back to them. You are told where it stands.

  4. Repair

    Parts are ordered and the work is done to the manufacturer's procedure. Aluminum work moves into the isolated clean room with its own dedicated tooling.

  5. Quality check

    Structural measurements verified, panel fit and alignment checked, finish inspected. If something is not right it does not leave.

  6. Pickup

    We call you when it is ready, walk the repair with you, and go over what was done before you drive it off.
