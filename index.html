What we do

Services

Collision work, start to finish, including the aluminum repairs a lot of shops in the area have to send somewhere else.

Collision repair

Impact damage brought back to the way the manufacturer built it. Panel repair, panel replacement, structural work, and the measuring that proves it is straight.

Gold Class

Aluminum structural repair

Isolated clean room, aluminum-only tooling, and manufacturer-specific training on seven makes. This is the work the certification is for.

Insurance claims

We write the damage, document it, and work the claim with your carrier so you are not the one translating body shop language over the phone.

Paint and refinishing

Color matched to your vehicle, blended into the adjacent panels, and cured so the repaired area is not the first thing someone notices.

Dent and bumper repair

Door dings, creased fenders, cracked and scuffed bumper covers. Smaller jobs still get the same prep and the same paint process.

Free written estimates

Bring the vehicle by during business hours and leave with the damage written up in writing. No charge. One note: if it is raining, we do not do estimates.
