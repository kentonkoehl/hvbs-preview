What happens next

  1. We read what you sent and call or email you back.
  2. You bring the vehicle to the shop during business hours so it can actually be looked at. A photo cannot show what is behind a panel.
  3. We write the damage up and hand you the estimate in writing, at no charge.
  4. You decide. Nothing is repaired until you give written authorization.

If it is raining, we do not do estimates. Water on the panels hides dents and hides paint damage. On a wet day, call first.
