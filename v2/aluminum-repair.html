Ask about your vehicle

Aluminum is not steel.

Fix it like steel and you find out later. Each manufacturer writes its own aluminum repair procedure, and seven of them certified this shop.

Steel dust ruins it.

Steel particles on bare aluminum start corrosion. It shows up as bubbling paint months after the repair.

Its own room.

Aluminum work belongs in an enclosed space, away from steel grinding.

Its own tools.

Sanders, hammers, welder, rivet gun. None of them touch steel.

The factory's book.

How far it can be worked, when a part gets replaced, how it gets joined. The manufacturer decides, not the tech.
