About

A family shop in Hardin Valley

Hardin Valley Body Shop, Inc. is family owned and operated. Mark Ogle is President and Owner, Sonya Ogle is Owner, and their son Dylan works in the business.

The Ogle family

When you call the shop you are talking to the people who own it. Mark Ogle is President and Owner. Sonya Ogle is Owner. Their son Dylan works here too.

That is the whole pitch, and it is the part that is hard for a bigger operation to copy. There is no regional manager to escalate to, no corporate script, and nobody passing your vehicle off to the next person in a chain. If something is wrong with a repair, the owner is standing right there and his name is on the building.

The certifications back it up. I-CAR Gold Class is the highest training recognition I-CAR awards, and the shop carries manufacturer aluminum certification on seven makes. 4.9 stars across 357 reviews. BBB A+ rated.
